Datamosh is a camera for creating live datamoshed video.

Record a take as a sequence of hold-to-record segments. Add datamosh segments to
carry motion from earlier footage into the next segment, or add a clean segment
to reset the effect. Choose the resolution, frame rate, and supported video
codec before beginning a take, and optionally use the inverted preview filter.

Finished videos are saved to the Movies/Datamosh folder in your device gallery.
They can be previewed, shared, or deleted from the app.

Datamosh uses the camera and microphone only while recording. It has no account,
advertising, analytics, or network features; recordings remain on your device.
